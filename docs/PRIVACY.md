# Privacy and safety

NatureQuest AI keeps data local by default.

- Journal entries are stored on the device unless a user explicitly shares them
- Observations should avoid precise locations by default
- Sharing consent must be explicit before any sync occurs
- No mandatory user account is required for the local-first experience
- AI outputs are not used as proof of species certainty or environmental impact

## Safety guidelines

- Never touch or disturb wildlife
- Stay on permitted paths and avoid hazardous or restricted areas
- Do not encourage collecting or handling unknown plants or insects
- Make uncertainty visible for identification results
